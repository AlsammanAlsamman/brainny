10. Growth rings (radial)

One concentric ring per domain, ideas placed as points around it in capture order, sized by evidence weight. Ties directly into the tree metaphor already in brAInny's own copy ("growth rings") — reads as a literal cross-section of the brain.

Adapted for this demo: the most natural version rings by session (each full lap = one working session); this one-session dataset has nothing to show there, so domains form the rings instead — still genuinely useful, and worth revisiting as session-rings once there's real multi-session history to show.
